# Product Page Trust Optimization

Customers decide in seconds whether they trust a product page.

## Key Trust Elements

### 1. Clear Product Description
Explain features, benefits, and expectations clearly.

### 2. Real Images
Use high-quality, realistic product images.

### 3. Customer Reviews
Display authentic reviews and ratings.

### 4. Delivery Information
Clearly mention shipping time and process.

### 5. Return Policy
Make return and refund policies easy to understand.

## Common Mistakes

- vague descriptions  
- hidden shipping timelines  
- no social proof  
- unclear policies  

## Result

Better trust = higher conversion rate.
