# Chargeback Prevention in Shopify Stores

Chargebacks are one of the most expensive problems in eCommerce.

They don’t just result in lost revenue — they damage payment processor trust and increase operational risk.

## Why Chargebacks Happen

Most chargebacks are not fraud.

They are caused by:

- unclear delivery timelines  
- lack of communication  
- delayed support responses  
- customers feeling ignored  

## How to Prevent Chargebacks

### 1. Clear Communication
Set accurate expectations before and after purchase.

### 2. Fast Response Time
Respond within 2–4 hours to reduce escalation risk.

### 3. Proactive Updates
Send order and shipping updates automatically.

### 4. Easy Support Access
Make it easy for customers to contact support.

### 5. Structured Dispute Handling
Always respond to disputes with:

- order confirmation  
- tracking details  
- communication logs  

## Key Insight

Chargebacks are rarely payment issues.

They are **customer experience failures**.
