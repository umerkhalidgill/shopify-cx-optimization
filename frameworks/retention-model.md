# Shopify Retention Model

Retention is the key driver of long-term eCommerce growth.

## Core Principle

Acquisition brings customers.  
Retention builds revenue.

## Retention Model Framework

### 1. Trust Layer
- Clear product information
- Transparent policies
- Visible reviews

### 2. Communication Layer
- Fast response time
- Order updates
- Clear messaging

### 3. Experience Layer
- Smooth support interactions
- Empathy-based communication
- Problem resolution

### 4. Loyalty Layer
- Repeat purchase incentives
- Post-purchase engagement
- Customer satisfaction

## Why It Matters

Improving retention by even a small percentage can significantly increase profitability.

Retention is not marketing.

It is a **system**.
